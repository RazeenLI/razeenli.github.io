MACSYS Student & EMCR Workshop and Centre Launch

Oct 27-28, 2025 · Parkville VIC 3010

MACSYS Student & EMCR Workshop (Oct 27)

A full-day workshop at the University of Melbourne where MACSYS students and EMCRs networked, shared research ideas, and explored opportunities for future collaboration, followed by dinner and mini golf.

MACSYS Centre Launch (Oct 28)

A formal launch event celebrating MACSYS’s collaborative research mission, featuring a headquarters tour, Welcome to Country and Smoking Ceremony, keynote speeches, and networking at the Science Gallery in Melbourne Connect.
